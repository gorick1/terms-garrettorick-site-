Services

A personal SMS notification service built on top of my own home automation setup. Once opted in, a phone number receives three kinds of messages:

Home-automation alerts

Triggered by events in my home automation setup — e.g. a sensor event or a security alert.

Reminders

Scheduled reminders configured by the recipient.

Status updates

Status updates from the self-hosted systems I run.
