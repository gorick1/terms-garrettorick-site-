Services

Subscribers receive regular automated notifications from our servers. Once opted in, a phone number receives three kinds of messages:

Alerts

Sent automatically when a monitored event occurs — e.g. a sensor event or a security alert.

Reminders

Scheduled reminders configured by the subscriber.

Status updates

Status updates on the systems and services our servers monitor.

This is a notification service — it does not send marketing or promotional messages. See SMS Opt-In to sign up, or Terms and Conditions for the full messaging terms.
