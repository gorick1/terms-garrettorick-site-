About

Based in Greeley, Colorado — building and running my own home automation systems.

I design and run my own home automation systems — things like Home Assistant setups, self-hosted tools, and small notification services that keep me on top of what's happening at home.

This site exists to document and support the personal SMS notification service tied to that setup: a system I built myself that texts alerts, reminders, and status updates to numbers that opt in. It's operated as a sole proprietorship by Garrett Orick — a personal, self-hosted service, not a commercial product sold to the public.
