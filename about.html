About

An SMS notification service operated by Garrett Orick in Greeley, Colorado.

The service delivers automated SMS notifications from our servers to subscribers who opt in: event alerts, scheduled reminders, and system status updates.

It is operated as a sole proprietorship by Garrett Orick. This site documents the service, its opt-in process, and the terms and privacy practices that apply to subscribers.
