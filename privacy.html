Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the SMS notification service operated by Garrett Orick (sole proprietor) handles subscriber information.

Information Collected

When you opt in, we collect the phone number you provide, along with the date, time, and IP address of your opt-in as a record of consent. This information is used to send the SMS alerts, reminders, and status updates you subscribed to.

Use of Information

Phone numbers used with this service are used solely to deliver messages the recipient opted in to receive. The service does not send marketing or promotional messages.

Data Sharing

No data collected through this service is sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Opt-Out

Recipients may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, or HELP for assistance.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact: contact@garrettorick.com
