This SMS notification service is a personal, self-hosted system operated by Garrett Orick (sole proprietor) for personal home-automation notification purposes.

The service may send SMS messages containing home-automation alerts, reminders, or status update messages to a phone number provided by the recipient during opt-in. No personal information is collected from or shared with third parties.

Phone numbers used with this service are used solely to deliver messages the recipient opted in to receive. This is not a marketing or commercial messaging service.

Data Sharing

No data collected through this service is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

Opt-Out

Recipients may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, or HELP for assistance.
